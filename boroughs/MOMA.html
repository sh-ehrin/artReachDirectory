Museum
The Museum
of Modern Art

MoMA is home to one of the world's most influential collections of modern and contemporary art, featuring over 200,000 works across painting, sculpture, film, design, and photography. Located in the heart of Midtown Manhattan, it remains one of the most visited art museums in the world.

MoMA's education department offers robust programming for school groups, including guided gallery tours aligned with NYC curriculum standards, hands-on artmaking workshops, and free educator resources to prepare students before their visit.

Student Programs

MoMA offers free admission for NYC public school students and teachers. Their 'MoMA for Schools' program provides curated gallery experiences and curriculum-connected visits for students at all grade levels. Pre-visit materials are available on their website.
